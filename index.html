How It Works

Three steps to
start earning

No minimum followers. No complicated setup. Just post your outfit and start earning from every sale.

01
📸
Post Your Outfit
Upload a photo or short video of your look. Morning OOTD, airport fit, Goa trip, gym session, date night — any outfit on any day counts.
02
🏷️
Tag Every Item
Paste any Myntra, Amazon, Ajio or Flipkart product link. Fitd auto-generates your personal affiliate link in one tap — no dashboard, no complexity.
